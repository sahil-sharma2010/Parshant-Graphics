05 • OUR WORK PROCESS

How We Build Your Signboard: Design, Manufacturing & Installation

A streamlined, transparent 3-step factory workflow from initial storefront measurement and 3D design to precision fabrication and on-site fitting.

STEP 01

1. Site Survey & 3D Design

Share your shop photo or book an on-site measurement. Our designers prepare a custom 3D board layout, help you choose the right acrylic/metal/ACP finish, and finalize exact dimensions.

  • Storefront measurement & consultation
  • Custom 3D typography & logo layout
  • Material & LED colour selection
STEP 02

2. In-House Manufacturing

Your signboard is fabricated at our Jind 3D Light Board Factory using precision laser cutting, CNC ACP grooving, 3D channel bending, and high-lumen waterproof LED matrix wiring.

  • Laser-cut 3D acrylic & stainless steel letters
  • Heavy MS iron framing & 4mm ACP cladding
  • Full illumination & weatherproofing test
STEP 03

3. Delivery & Installation

Our experienced fitting team safely transports the board to your location, performs heavy-duty structural mounting, connects weatherproof power supplies, and hands over a glowing storefront.

  • Safe transport to your shop or gate site
  • Crane / scaffolding & secure fastener fitting
  • Clean electrical setup & warranty handover
